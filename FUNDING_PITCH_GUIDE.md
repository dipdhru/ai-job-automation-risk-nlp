## Red Flags to Avoid

❌ **Don't say:** "This is like LinkedIn + Coursera combined"  
✅ **Do say:** "We're the first to quantify individual job automation risk"

❌ **Don't say:** "We'll target everyone (consumers, HR, companies, governments)"  
✅ **Do say:** "We're starting with job seekers, then expanding to enterprise"

❌ **Don't say:** "Our model is accurate 99%"  
✅ **Do say:** "Our NLP model correlates with O*NET data with 87% accuracy"

❌ **Don't say:** "We have no competitors"  
✅ **Do say:** "We're the first to solve this specific problem this way"
