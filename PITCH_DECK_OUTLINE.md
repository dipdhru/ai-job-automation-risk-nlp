## 🎬 How to Create This in Google Slides

1. Go to **slides.google.com** → New Presentation
2. Choose a professional template (e.g., "Focus", "Modern Writer")
3. Copy slide content from this outline
4. For each slide:
   - Add the text in your speaker notes
   - Search for stock images (Unsplash integration)
   - Keep design clean: 1 headline + 3-4 bullets per slide
5. Practice out loud 20 times
6. Time yourself (should take 10-12 minutes total)

## 🗣️ Delivery Tips

### What Investors Look For:
✓ **Problem clarity** - Does the problem resonate?  
✓ **Solution differentiation** - Why you vs. others?  
✓ **Market size** - Is it big enough?  
✓ **Traction** - Have you validated this?  
✓ **Team credibility** - Can you execute?  
✓ **Financial realism** - Do numbers make sense?  

### Delivery Style:
- **Speak conversationally**, not reading slides
- **Tell stories**, not just facts
- **Show passion** for the problem
- **Admit constraints** (builds credibility)
- **End with a question** to start dialogue

### Common Mistakes to Avoid:
❌ Too much text per slide  
❌ Trying to explain everything  
❌ No clear ask or CTA  
❌ Unrealistic financial projections  
❌ Talking at people instead of to them
