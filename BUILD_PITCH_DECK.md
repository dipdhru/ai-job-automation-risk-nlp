## 🎯 Step-by-Step

### **STEP 1: Create Google Slides**

1. Go to **https://docs.google.com/presentation**
2. Click **"New Presentation"**
3. Name it: `AI Job Risk Analyzer - Pitch`
4. Choose template: **"Focus"** or **"Modern Writer"** (professional templates)

---

### **STEP 2: Use Your Outline**

File: `PITCH_DECK_OUTLINE.md` in your repo

This has all the content and speaker notes for each slide!

**Copy structure for each slide:**
1. **Title slide** - Problem hook
2. **Problem** - 3 statistics
3. **Market** - TAM/SAM/SOM breakdown
4. **Solution** - Demo flow explanation
5. **Competition** - How you're different
6. **Business Model** - Pricing tiers
7. **Traction** - Proof points
8. **Go-to-Market** - 6-month plan
9. **Team** - Who you are
10. **The Ask** - Funding needed + use

---

### **STEP 3: Add Content to Each Slide**

For each slide in Google Slides:

1. Add title
2. Add bullet points
3. Add data/numbers
4. Add speaker notes (View → Speaker notes)

---

### **STEP 4: Add Visuals**

**Google Slides has built-in image search:**

1. Click **"Image"** → **"Search the web"**
2. Search for:
   - "AI automation" - for problem section
   - "Jobs" - for market section
   - "Growth chart" - for traction
   - "Team" - for team slide
3. Insert and adjust sizing

**Use Unsplash for free images:**
- https://unsplash.com
- Download and upload to slides

---

### **STEP 5: Color & Branding**

**Match your app colors:**

```
Primary: #667eea (Purple-blue)
Secondary: #764ba2 (Purple)
Accent: #51cf66 (Green for good)
Warning: #ff6b6b (Red for risk)
```

**Apply throughout:**
- Title backgrounds
- Highlight boxes
- Charts/graphs
- Call-to-action buttons

---

### **STEP 6: Add Charts (If Needed)**

For financial projections:

1. Click **"Chart"** → **"Table"**
2. Create 3-year projection table:

```
                Year 1      Year 2      Year 3
Users           2,000       15,000      50,000
Pro Users       50          500         4,000
Revenue         $15K        $200K       $800K
```

Or use built-in charts for visuals

---

### **STEP 7: Speaker Notes**

Add notes for each slide (these don't show to audience):

1. View → **"Speaker notes"**
2. Add talking points for each slide
3. Include:
   - What to say
   - How long (60 sec, etc)
   - Key points to emphasize
   - Stories to tell

---

### **STEP 8: Practice**

1. **Slideshow mode** - Press `Shift + F5`
2. **Read speaker notes** - Practice talking
3. **Time yourself** - Aim for 10-12 minutes
4. **Refine** - Adjust slides based on flow

## 🎨 Design Tips

**Keep it simple:**
- 1 headline + 3-4 bullets per slide
- Big numbers, small text
- 1 image per slide minimum
- Consistent fonts (use 2 max)

**Visual hierarchy:**
- Large headlines (36-44pt)
- Medium bullets (24-28pt)
- Speaker notes (12pt)

**Color usage:**
- Use your brand colors consistently
- Red for problems, Green for solutions
- White space is your friend

---

## 🎤 Practice Tips

**Before your event:**
- [ ] Record yourself presenting (watch it)
- [ ] Practice 20+ times (out loud!)
- [ ] Time each slide (aim for 1-2 min)
- [ ] Prepare answers to common questions
- [ ] Have backup screenshots
- [ ] Print speaker notes (physical backup)

**During presentation:**
- Speak clearly and slowly
- Look at audience, not slides
- Tell stories, not just facts
- Pause for questions
- Show passion!

## 📞 Common Questions

**Q: How long should it be?**
A: 10-12 minutes for a full pitch
2-3 minutes for elevator pitch

**Q: Should I memorize?**
A: Know your story, don't memorize word-for-word
Read speaker notes, stay flexible

**Q: What if someone asks a question?**
A: Pause, answer, then continue
It's a conversation, not a monologue

**Q: What if tech fails?**
A: Have backup:
- Screenshot of each slide (email)
- PDF (email or USB)
- Print (physical backup)
